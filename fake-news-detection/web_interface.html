❓ Understanding Confidence Levels

▼

The classifier gives each prediction a confidence score from 0% to 100%. Here's what those numbers mean:

90% — 100%

Very High Confidence

The model is very certain. Strong signals (like conspiracy keywords or official sources) make this an easy call. These predictions are highly reliable.

70% — 89%

High Confidence

The model is fairly certain. Most predictions land here. Reliable for general screening, but borderline cases may need a quick glance.

50% — 69%

Moderate Confidence

The model sees some signals but is less sure. Short texts, neutral topics, or mixed signals can cause this. Best to read the article yourself.

Below 50%

Low Confidence

The model is essentially guessing. This happens with very short text, unusual topics, or content the model hasn't learned well. Treat with caution.

💡 Tip: Use the threshold slider to control how strict the classifier is. At 0.60 (default), most reasonable predictions pass. Raise it to 0.80+ if you only want the most reliable results. The Compare tab lets you see how the ML model and keyword classifier differ — when they agree, you can be more confident in the result.
